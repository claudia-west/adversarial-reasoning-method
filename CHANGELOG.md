# Changelog

All notable changes to the ARM manuscript are documented here. Full per-line revision notes live in the manuscript's own Appendix A; this file tracks substantive, public-facing changes only — not editorial or copyediting passes.

# v1.7 — September 2026

### Conceptual changes
- Reframed the knowledge-vs-reasoning relationship as an interaction model rather than a dichotomy (Abstract, Section 1.2, Section 13).
- Repositioned Represent (Step 2) as an externalization/communication tool rather than a demonstrated accuracy intervention, per Bordage & Lemieux's own null causal findings.
- Shifted the paper's stated primary contribution toward the Reflect/Integrate stages.
- Added an explicit, unresolved limitation: whether procedural reasoning frameworks are the right unit of analysis for clinical reasoning at all.

### Structural additions
- Added Section 2.7 (Deliberate Reflection / Mamede & Schmidt) and Section 2.8 (positioning relative to retrospective error-analysis curricula).
- Added Section 2.9 (comparison to SNAPPS and the Assessment of Reasoning Tool).
- Added Section 9.1 (case-volume/depth tradeoff, distinct from documentation burden) and reframed rigor/throughput tiering as a research question.

### Pilot design
- Narrowed the Section 8 pilot's near-miss comparison to a within-rater design (ARM output vs. the same rater's own initial pre-ARM impression), since the single-arm design cannot support a between-groups claim.
- Rewrote Step 5 (Challenge) and its framework-overview row around expected impact on the leading management decision, rather than breadth of hypotheses moved.

### Literature
- Removed the "name the biases" instruction from Step 4; cited the bias-identification literature as the reason for removal.
- Completed an independent two-pass literature search; added current 2025–2026 sources.
